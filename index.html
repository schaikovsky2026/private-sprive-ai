The 80/20 Plan

Eighty percent QQQ, twenty percent gold, a fixed monthly contribution, and one rule: the money buys whichever side has fallen behind.

262 months · Jan 2005 – Oct 2026$1,000/month · $262,000 inQQQ + GLD only

The rules

  1. On the last trading day of each month, look at your account: what fraction of it is QQQ?
  2. Below 80%? Put this month's entire contribution into QQQ. At or above 80%? Put it all into GLD. (First month ever: split $800 / $200.)
  3. Never sell. Ignore drift in the first 12 months, when a single deposit can swing a small account. After that, if QQQ ever goes below 70% or above 90% of the account, do a one-time true rebalance back to 80/20. In this backtest that never happened.
